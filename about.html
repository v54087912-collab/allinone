About Toolora

Toolora was created to solve a pervasive problem on the modern internet: simple image editing utilities that secretly harvest personal photos, charge deceptive subscriptions, throttle download speeds, or stamp ugly watermarks on your work.

1

Zero Server Storage

Every crop, background cutout, and compression calculation executes entirely inside your browser sandbox. We run zero backend file storage servers.

2

WASM & HTML5

Powered by modern WebAssembly neural runtimes and GPU-accelerated HTML5 Canvas, providing desktop-grade speed without downloading heavy desktop software.

3

Free Forever

Monetized non-intrusively via standard Google AdSense display advertisements, keeping all tools 100% free with unlimited batch processing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my tasks and notes saved if I refresh the page?

Yes! Your data is saved locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage, so your plans remain intact even after closing the browser.

Does Toolora sync my data to a cloud account?

No cloud sync is needed, ensuring 100% privacy and zero risk of external data leaks.

Can I export my tasks or schedule?

Yes, you can export your notes, tasks, or calendar files (.ics) directly to your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, all productivity tools operate seamlessly without an internet connection.
