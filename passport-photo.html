Passport Photo Standards Reference

🇺🇸 United States (2×2 inches)

Required for US Passports, Green Cards, and Visas. Dimensions are 51×51 mm (600×600 px @ 300 DPI) with head height between 1 inch and 1 3/8 inches.

🇬🇧 🇪🇺 UK & Schengen (35×45 mm)

Standard biometric passport photo for the UK, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, and India. Head length between 29 mm and 34 mm.
