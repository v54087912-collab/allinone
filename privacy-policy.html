Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

1. Complete Client-Side Image Privacy Guarantee

At Toolora, your privacy is paramount. Unlike conventional web applications, we do not upload, transmit, store, or view your image files on any web server. All operations—including bulk image cropping, AI background removal, and file compression—occur strictly on your personal device using HTML5 Canvas, the JavaScript File API, and local WebAssembly (WASM) execution.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and device identifiers to collect non-personally identifiable information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide targeted advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

Toolora uses your browser's localStorage to remember non-sensitive configuration settings:

  • toolora_theme: Stores your light or dark mode selection.
  • quickpic_cookie_consent: Records whether you accepted or customized cookie notifications.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we never collect, harvest, or sell personal identifiers or image files, users under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) benefit from built-in privacy by design.

5. Updates & Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my tasks and notes saved if I refresh the page?

Yes! Your data is saved locally on your device using HTML5 LocalStorage, so your plans remain intact even after closing the browser.

Does Toolora sync my data to a cloud account?

No cloud sync is needed, ensuring 100% privacy and zero risk of external data leaks.

Can I export my tasks or schedule?

Yes, you can export your notes, tasks, or calendar files (.ics) directly to your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, all productivity tools operate seamlessly without an internet connection.
