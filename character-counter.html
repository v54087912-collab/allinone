Frequently Asked Questions

Why does UTF-8 byte count differ from character count?

Standard ASCII characters use 1 byte, while emojis and non-Latin scripts use 2 to 4 bytes per character.

What is an SMS segment?

Standard GSM-7 text messages allow 160 characters per SMS part.
