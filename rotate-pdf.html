Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF & Document tool free to use?

Yes, Rotate PDF Pages on Toolora is 100% free with no subscriptions, sign-ups, or hidden limits.

Are my confidential documents uploaded to any server?

Never. All file parsing, encryption, editing, and conversion happen entirely in your local browser using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript.

Can I use Rotate PDF Pages offline on mobile or desktop?

Yes, once loaded, Toolora works completely offline without an active internet connection on Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android.

Standard PDF versions 1.4-2.0, Microsoft Word (.docx), plain text (.txt), and Markdown documents.
