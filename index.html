Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our privacy-first client-side image editor.

Are my images uploaded to any server? ↓

No, absolutely never. All image reading, cropping, background removal, and compression happen entirely inside your web browser via HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly (WASM), and the Web Streams API. We have no backend image processing servers, which guarantees 100% privacy.

Is there any file limit or watermark added? ↓

There are no watermarks, no account registration requirements, and no arbitrary daily quotas. You can crop or compress hundreds of images simultaneously and download them in a convenient ZIP archive.

How does the background remover work without a server? ↓

We execute a lightweight neural segmentation model compiled to WebAssembly (WASM) directly on your device's CPU/GPU via your browser. If you go offline or are on a constrained connection, our intelligent local edge-matte algorithm takes over instantly.

Can I use these tools completely offline? ↓

Yes! Once this website is loaded in your browser tab, all JavaScript code is stored in your local browser cache. You can disconnect your internet completely and continue cropping, compressing, and removing backgrounds without interruptions.
