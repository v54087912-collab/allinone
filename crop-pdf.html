Why Crop PDFs with Toolora?

✂️ Lossless CropBox Modification

Our tool directly alters the official PDF CropBox and MediaBox parameters. No rasterization occurs, keeping all text, bookmarks, and links intact.

📖 Odd & Even Facing Pages

Ideal for scanned books and documents where binding margins alternate between left and right pages.

🔒 100% In-Browser Privacy

Your documents never leave your computer. Everything runs locally in pure WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, PDF Page Cropper on Toolora is 100% free with no subscriptions, sign-ups, or hidden limits.

Never. All file parsing, encryption, editing, and conversion happen entirely in your local browser using client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript.

Yes, once loaded, Toolora works completely offline without an active internet connection on Windows, Mac, iOS, and Android.
