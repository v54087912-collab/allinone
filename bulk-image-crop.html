How to Crop Multiple Assets from One Image

Step 1

Upload or Drop Image

Drag and drop any high-resolution JPG, PNG, WEBP, or SVG image, or select a demo RPG sprite sheet to experiment.

Step 2

Draw & Add Crops

Click and drag on the canvas to draw crop boundaries, or use the Grid tool to auto-generate rows and columns of equal sizes.

Step 3

Fine-Tune & Manage

Use the Asset sidebar to rename, reorder, preview, or remove any crop definition. Toggle background removal if needed.

Step 4

Export All or ZIP

Click Export to download your assets in PNG, WebP, or JPEG formats at full resolution packaged neatly in a ZIP archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I define more than one crop on the same image? ↓

Yes, absolutely! MultiCrop is purpose-built to let you create unlimited crop rectangles on a single photo or graphic sheet. Each crop can have its own dimensions, aspect ratio, and custom filename.

Is my image data kept private? ↓

100% private. All processing takes place directly in your web browser via HTML5 Canvas. Your images are never sent to a cloud server or external API.

Can I switch to Fullscreen mode for more workspace? ↓

Yes! Look for the Fullscreen icon (Maximize) in the top right of the MultiCrop studio navigation bar. Clicking it toggles a distraction-free full-screen studio view.

What formats can I export my crops in? ↓

You can export cropped assets as transparent PNG, lossy or lossless WebP, or high-quality JPEG. You can download individual crops or package all crops into a single ZIP archive.

Does this tool work on mobile devices? ↓

Yes! Toolora MultiCrop Studio includes touch gestures, pan, zoom, and a slide-out mobile asset drawer for phones and tablets.
